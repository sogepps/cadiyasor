Used the polite/plural verb and imperative forms (ይጠብቁ, ያድርጉ, ይግለጹ, ...) throughout instead of the gendered informal 2nd-person singular (አንተ/አንቺ), which keeps the witch's address to the player gender-neutral and also reads as normal, warm register for an app aimed at everyone. Chose "አስማተኛ" (magician) over "ጠንቋይ" (witch/sorcerer) because the latter carries a real-world witchcraft-accusation connotation in Ethiopian usage that would clash with the light, storybook tone the brief asks for; the witch character herself is marked feminine ("አስማተኛዋ") since that's simply her character, not the player's gender. Kept opposite pairs opposite (heart/head, harder/easier path, take/don't take the risk) and avoided any real religious wording.
